<br>

Body to body, form to form, my reality changing between each. The information written inside of each being, augmenting the views of my sight, the shape of my being, the urge to create or decimate intertwining with my consciousness. Traversing the crevices of all entities, each one with a part to play and a story to follow. Whether carefully crafted by self or made by the dozen for consumption.

I want to experience it.

I want to caress the mere premise of existing, to guide my hands through the threads, to feel the pulsing beat of its form. The force that binds it together and yet promises infinite expansion. to learn the role of each thing, whatever it may be.

What if I could create my own form of that reality? I could give a rebirth to existence, an understanding that all can experience. to dwell in that pleasure and share it to those that haven't bore witness to its beauty. To pull the strings together into ropes and knots, connecting the premises of creation and form.

To alter a body to contain every piece of existence. Solidifying the soul into something tangible and giving it the sight of everything. So every being can appreciate the love and effort I can see.
